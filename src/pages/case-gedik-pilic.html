Gedik Piliç

Building brand engagement through creative social media strategy.

Consumer goods

01

Client overview

Gedik Piliç is one of Turkey’s well-established food brands, offering a wide range of high-quality chicken products designed for everyday consumption.

With a strong presence in the Turkish food industry, the brand set out to strengthen its relationship with consumers by delivering accessible, inspiring and engaging food experiences across digital platforms.

FKR Media partnered with Gedik Piliç to enhance its social media presence, increase audience engagement, and develop a consistent digital communication strategy that connects the brand with modern consumers.

02

The challenge

In fast-moving consumer goods, brands need more than product visibility — they need meaningful connections with their audiences.

  • Strengthen brand presence across digital channels
  • Increase consumer engagement
  • Create consistent and creative content communication
  • Grow community interaction organically
  • Build stronger brand awareness among target audiences
03

Our strategy

Creative content strategy

A social media content strategy that put product communication and lifestyle storytelling in the same frame, rather than alternating between them.

What we made

  • Product-focused creative posts
  • Recipe inspirations
  • Seasonal campaigns
  • Consumer-oriented storytelling
  • Brand awareness content

Social media growth management

Strategic content planning and platform-specific communication across Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn.

The Instagram community grew from 13.7K followers at the start of the period to 14.6K by the end of June 2026 — consistent growth rather than a single campaign spike.

Performance-driven advertising

Digital advertising was integrated into the social strategy rather than run beside it, to expand content reach, increase visibility and support audience acquisition.

Through Q1, advertising activity contributed to content reach and visibility performance.

Influencer & community communication

Influencer collaborations and community-driven content were brought into the brand communication strategy to strengthen consumer interaction.

Between January and June 2026, 197 original pieces of content were published, supported by 46 influencer content shares.

06

Impact

Through strategic content production, creative storytelling, influencer communication and performance-driven digital marketing, FKR Media helped Gedik Piliç strengthen its digital ecosystem.

The collaboration turned social media channels into active brand communication platforms, increasing consumer engagement and creating a stronger connection between the brand and its audience.
