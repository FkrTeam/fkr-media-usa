Bizim Evler

Accelerating real estate growth through data-driven digital marketing.

Residential development

01

Client overview

Bizim Evler is a leading residential development brand operating under İhlas Holding, one of Turkey’s well-established and diversified business groups.

İhlas Holding has a strong presence across construction, media, manufacturing, energy, healthcare and services. Decades of experience and a strong reputation in the Turkish market stand behind numerous successful projects, each combining quality, trust and modern living standards.

FKR Media partnered with Bizim Evler to strengthen its digital presence, increase qualified customer acquisition, and create a scalable performance marketing strategy supporting its residential projects.

02

The challenge

In the competitive real estate industry, success depends on reaching the right audience with the right message at the right time.

Bizim Evler needed a comprehensive digital growth strategy, and it had to answer five things at once.

  • Increase awareness of residential projects
  • Reach potential buyers with high purchase intent
  • Generate qualified lead opportunities
  • Strengthen digital visibility and brand positioning
  • Transform advertising investment into measurable business results
03

Our strategy

Performance marketing & lead generation

Data-driven advertising built to reach potential homebuyers through advanced audience targeting, behavioural analysis and continuous campaign optimisation.

The objective was never volume alone. The work was aimed at qualified prospects with genuine interest in a residential project, which is a different audience from the one a reach buy delivers.

Video marketing strategy

YouTube advertising carried the lifestyle experience behind the Bizim Evler projects — the part of a development that a floor plan cannot communicate.

What the film showed

  • Modern living spaces
  • Project advantages
  • Social facilities
  • Brand trust and experience

Large-scale digital reach

Google Display advertising expanded project visibility across a wide digital ecosystem and reached millions of potential customers.

Data-driven optimisation

Every campaign was monitored continuously and optimised against performance insight rather than left to run to plan.

What we analysed

  • Audience behaviour
  • Conversion performance
  • Creative effectiveness
  • Campaign efficiency

Impact
