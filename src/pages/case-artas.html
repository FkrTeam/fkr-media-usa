Artaş

Turning digital advertising into measurable real estate results.

Residential development

01

Client overview

Artaş is one of Turkey’s most recognised real estate developers, known for creating large-scale residential projects with strong architectural identity and investment value.

FKR Media worked with Artaş to enhance digital visibility, reach qualified audiences, and support sales performance through strategic online campaigns.

02

The challenge

The real estate industry requires more than visibility — it requires reaching the right audience with the right message.

  • Increase project awareness
  • Generate qualified customer interest
  • Improve digital campaign efficiency
  • Support sales performance through measurable marketing activity
03

Our strategy

Audience-focused digital campaigns

Targeted advertising strategies built on user behaviour, interests, demographics and location data.

Video advertising

YouTube campaigns highlighted project experiences and lifestyle benefits, to create stronger emotional connections with potential buyers.

Performance optimisation

Campaign performance was monitored continuously, which allowed budgets, audiences and creatives to be optimised for better results.

Data-driven growth

Every campaign decision was supported by performance insight and measurable data.

Impact

Through strategic digital advertising and performance-focused campaign management, FKR Media helped Artaş connect with potential buyers more effectively.

Digital attention became measurable business outcomes — the sales the campaign is finally judged on, rather than the reach it was bought with.
