Kervan

Building brand visibility and engagement through strategic social media management.

Furniture

01

Client overview

Kervan is one of Turkey’s established furniture brands, offering stylish, high-quality and functional furniture designed to enrich modern living spaces.

With a strong position in the home furniture sector, Kervan set out to strengthen its digital presence by creating meaningful connections with consumers and showing its products through inspiring visual communication.

FKR Media partnered with Kervan to enhance its social media performance, increase brand visibility, improve audience engagement, and build a stronger digital communication ecosystem across Facebook and Instagram.

02

The challenge

In a highly competitive furniture industry, brands need more than product visibility — they need strong storytelling, consistent communication and an engaging digital presence.

  • Increase brand awareness across digital platforms
  • Expand content reach and visibility
  • Strengthen audience interaction
  • Drive more profile visits and website traffic
  • Create a consistent and inspiring social media presence
  • Build stronger relationships with potential customers
03

Our strategy

Creative content strategy

A communication strategy that put product presentation and lifestyle-driven storytelling in the same frame.

Positioning Kervan not only as a furniture provider but as a source of inspiration for living spaces gave the brand something to say between product launches — and that is where the emotional connection with an audience is actually made.

What we made

  • Furniture-focused creative content
  • Interior inspiration posts
  • Product storytelling
  • Lifestyle-oriented visuals
  • Brand awareness campaigns
  • Engagement-driven social formats

Social media growth management

Strategic content planning, platform optimisation and consistent communication across Facebook and Instagram.

Instagram reached 51.6M total views and 5.9M reach against 351.2K profile visits; Facebook carried 27.2M total views and 41.1K profile visits. Two platforms, two audiences, planned separately rather than fed the same posts.

Performance-driven digital growth

Paid and organic performance were run as one strategy rather than two, to maximise content visibility and audience interaction.

What it moved

  • Content discovery
  • Engagement rates
  • Audience acquisition
  • Traffic from social platforms

Growth over the previous period

  • Instagram link clicks +236.2%
  • Instagram interactions +373.2%
  • Facebook link clicks +138.9%
  • Facebook interactions +98%

05

Platform performance

Instagram

Instagram became Kervan’s strongest digital growth channel.

51.6 million total views, 5.9 million reach, 351.2 thousand profile visits and 1.6 million link clicks, with content interactions up 373.2%. The change is what those numbers describe: the account stopped being a product showcase and started being where customers find the brand and act on it.

Facebook

Facebook carried brand awareness and sent traffic on.

27.2 million total views, 41.1 thousand profile visits, 183.5 thousand link clicks, 7.4 thousand content interactions and 792 new followers. A smaller audience than Instagram’s, doing a different job: widening who can reach the brand and pushing them toward Kervan’s own touchpoints.

06

Impact

Through strategic content production, creative storytelling and performance-focused social media management, FKR Media helped Kervan strengthen its digital ecosystem.

The collaboration turned social platforms into brand communication channels, generating millions of impressions, increasing audience interaction and creating stronger connections between Kervan and its customers.

Creative content and data-driven optimisation together produced growth that holds, and reinforced Kervan’s position as an inspiring furniture brand online.
