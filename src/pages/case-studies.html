01

Residential development

Bizim Evler

A residential development sells long before anyone can walk through it. The launch had to turn attention into named demand — not impressions, but people who had put their details down.

The brief

Build reach for a development at launch scale, and convert that reach into a register of interested buyers the sales team could work from.

What we ran

Reach first, capture second. TrueView carried the film to an audience that chose to watch it; Google Display held the development in front of the same audience at scale. Both pointed at one pre-registration form.

03

Public offering

Halka Arz

A public offering runs to a fixed date. Awareness has to arrive before the book opens, and it has to arrive interested — reach that nobody acts on is reach that arrived too late to matter.

The brief

Put the offering in front of a broad investor audience inside a fixed window, and be able to show that the audience engaged with it.

What we ran

A broad-reach buy against a hard deadline, with creative and targeting judged on click-through and engagement rather than on how many people the media plan could technically reach.
